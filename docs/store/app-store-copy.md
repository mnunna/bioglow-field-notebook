## Description

Turn your iPhone or iPad into a private, offline-first field notebook for ecological
observations.

BioGlow: Eco Note helps ecologists, students, and field teams record unexpected
discoveries without relying on an account or network connection. Choose a guided
template, capture the details that matter, and keep observations organized on the
device.

CAPTURE FIELD EVIDENCE

• Choose from six guided templates for plants, animals and reptiles, bioluminescent
organisms, carbon-cycle observations, quick photo and voice capture, or a general
field note.

• Add a photo, GPS position and accuracy, typed location, or recorded voice note.

• Save the observation time and date automatically, then edit, star, inspect, or
delete the entry later.

ORGANIZE YOUR FIELDWORK

• Keep new observations in History and move completed research batches into Archive
after export.

• Search and filter archived observations across Species, BioGlow, Carbon, and Daily
Field Journal notebooks.

• View usable GPS observations on an embedded map with category-coloured markers and
an accessible companion list.

EXPORT RESEARCH-READY RECORDS

• Create one research ZIP containing attached photos and audio, a readable
spreadsheet CSV, and a Darwin Core-compatible occurrence CSV.

• Share or save the archive using the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is uploaded
automatically.

BUILT FOR REAL FIELD CONDITIONS

• Rain / Glove Mode enlarges controls and text when wet hands or gloves make precise
taps difficult.

• Core capture, browsing, trivia, and export work without an account. Observations
and managed attachments stay on the device unless you explicitly export or share
them.

BioGlow: Eco Note includes no advertising, analytics, automatic cloud sync, or user
accounts.

### Notes for App Review

BioGlow: Eco Note is an offline-first field notebook. It does not require an account
and has no advertising, analytics, subscriptions, in-app purchases, cloud database,
or automatic cloud sync. Observations, photos, voice recordings, and location tags
remain on the device unless the user explicitly exports or shares a research ZIP.

To exercise the main workflow:

1. Open the **New note** tab.
2. Select **BioGlow Finding** or any other template.
3. Enter a title and the template details. Camera, photo-library, location, and
   microphone access are optional; denying any permission does not block a typed
   field note.
4. Optionally add a GPS position, photo, and voice note, then save the observation.
5. Open **History** to inspect or edit the saved entry.
6. Use **Observation map** to view entries with usable GPS coordinates.
7. Tap **Export research ZIP** to create a ZIP containing CSV files and available
   attachments. After a successful save or share, the app offers to move exported
   observations from History to Archive.
8. Open **More** for the offline usage guide, biodiversity trivia, feedback action,
   privacy policy, and support page.

The app has been tested on a physical iPhone. Before submission, replace this

supports iPad.
