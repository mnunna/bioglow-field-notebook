# BioGlow: Eco Note Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

BioGlow: Eco Note is an offline-first field notebook created by Mecha Masters. This
policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information stored on your device

Eco Note can store field observations, typed notes, dates and times, location tags,
photos, and voice recordings. This information is stored locally on your device. The
app has no user accounts, cloud database, advertising, analytics, or automatic cloud
sync, and Mecha Masters does not collect this information.

## Device permissions

Eco Note requests camera, photo-library, microphone, and location access only for the
features that need them. You can deny or later revoke these permissions in your
device settings. Core note-taking continues to work without them, including a typed
location fallback.

## Maps and platform services

The observation map uses the mapping service provided by the device platform. That
service may load map content under the platform provider's own terms and privacy
policy. Mecha Masters does not receive your map activity or field observations.

## Exporting, sharing, and feedback

Your observations remain on your device unless you explicitly export or share a
research archive. After export, the copy you create is handled by the destination
and sharing service you choose.

The app's feedback action opens your device's email app. If you choose to send the
message, its contents and your email address are sent to Mecha Masters and handled by
your email provider and ours. Do not include sensitive field data unless you intend
to share it with us.

## Retention and deletion

You control the observations stored by Eco Note. You can delete individual entries
and their managed attachments in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local app
data, subject to your device's backup and restore behavior. Copies that you exported
or shared must be deleted from their destinations separately.

## Changes to this policy

If Eco Note's data practices change, this policy and the app-store privacy disclosures
will be updated before the changed version is released.
