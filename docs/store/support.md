# BioGlow: Eco Note Support

Eco Note is an offline-first field notebook for recording ecological observations.

## Contact

For help, bug reports, or feedback, email
[mecha_masters@icloud.com](mailto:mecha_masters@icloud.com).

Please include the device model, operating-system version, and a short description of
what happened. Do not include sensitive field observations, precise locations,
photos, or voice recordings unless you intentionally want to share them with the
support team.

## Quick help

- Camera, photos, location, and voice recording require the matching permission in
  the device's Settings app.
- Notes are stored on the device and do not require an account or internet connection.
- Use Export to create a research ZIP before removing the app or moving to a new
  device.
- Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to the device's backup and restore
  behavior.

Privacy policy: `https://bioglow-discovery.vercel.app/privacy`
